Smart Meta-controller: Energy Efficient Dynamic Routing for Large Language Model Inference Systems

R. Jeffrey Mariaraj, Ronan Lobo, Maurya P. Gowda
2025 9th International Conference on Computational System and Information Technology for Sustainable Solutions · DOI 10.1109/CSITSS67709.2025.11295492
Paper (DOI) ↗Code ↗
Abstract, in brief

Serving every prompt with the largest model wastes energy on queries that do not need it. The Smart Meta-controller puts a fine-tuned DistilBERT classifier in front of two agents. It labels each prompt simple or complex in about 15 ms, sends simple prompts to a lightweight agent and complex ones to a deep agent, and lets the lightweight agent escalate when it detects it is out of its depth.

Contributions
  • —A two-stage routing architecture with a learned first-stage classifier
  • —An escalation protocol so a wrong first call can be corrected without failing the task
  • —A comparison of BERT, DistilBERT, DynaBERT and RoBERTa as the classifier backbone
  • —An energy and cost analysis of routing against sending everything to the deep agent

Question

XyloTron identifies wood species from macroscopic images. How much of its accuracy survives when the model is quantized to run entirely on a camera device with no network, and how much of the loss can fine-tuning recover?

Constraints
  • —Inference fully offline, on the device
  • —Memory and compute of an edge device, not a workstation GPU
  • —Identification quality judged against the original model
